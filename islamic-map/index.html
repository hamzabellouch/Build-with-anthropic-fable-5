About this map

This globe traces the geographic story of Islam — from the first revelation near Mecca in 610 CE to a worldwide community of roughly two billion people today.

Green areas show lands under Muslim rule and/or with Muslim-majority populations; pale areas show significant minorities, rule over largely non-Muslim populations, or strong trading influence. Gold dots are key events; dashed lines are the trade routes — Saharan, Silk Road and Indian Ocean — along which the faith often travelled farther than any army.

All boundaries and dates are approximate. Historical frontiers were zones, not lines; conversion of populations lagged conquest by centuries, and this visualization simplifies both. It is an educational sketch, not a scholarly atlas.

Base geography: Natural Earth via world-atlas. Modern percentages: Pew Research Center estimates. Historical extents and dates: standard reference historiography, hand-drawn for this visualization. Built with D3.js — everything runs locally in your browser.
